Who We Are

Founder-led QA for small SaaS teams

Precision QA Consulting is a specialized QA practice focused on helping small SaaS teams ship with confidence, especially when releases feel risky and quality signals are hard to trust.

Led by Hunter, with 13+ years of hands-on QA experience across web, mobile, and API platforms. Our approach is practical and focused: identify what actually matters, reduce noise in testing and CI, and introduce lightweight structure so teams can move faster without sacrificing quality.

When needed, we scale through a trusted network of senior QA professionals, ensuring the right level of coverage without adding unnecessary overhead.
