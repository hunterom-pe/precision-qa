Founder Story

THE PRECISION JOURNEY

Hunter Ortega-Mathews, Founder & Lead QA

With 13+ years rooted deeply in software testing, my career began crafting foundational test plans for custom enterprise software and scaling offshore test teams. Over a decade, that foundation grew into leading QA for massive tech migrations and conducting high-stakes mobile testing.

From ensuring 100% test coverage for university systems serving thousands, to participating in covert, live-environment field testing for Apple iOS betas, the reality remains the same: you cannot afford to release blindly.

I launched Precision QA to bring elite, enterprise-level testing insights directly to SaaS teams. You don't need heavy, slow processes; you need precision, clear signals, and the confidence to hit "deploy."
